# Hybrid Agent 1.2.0: simpler, Windows-first

Your own AI helpers on your own Windows PC: no account, no API key.

## Get started

1. Download `HybridAgent-1.2.0-windows-x64.zip` below and unzip it anywhere.
2. Double-click **HybridAgent.exe**.
3. Press **Set up now**. It picks a model that fits your PC and downloads it (one time, 1-9 GB).

Windows may warn "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed: **More info**, then
**Run anyway**. To check the download: `certutil -hashfile HybridAgent-1.2.0-windows-x64.zip SHA256`
and compare with `SHA256SUMS`.

## New in 1.2.0

- One-click setup, sized to your PC's memory, with a progress bar.
- Agents are plain ASCII smiley faces with small differences (Blue, Green, Orange); make your own.
- Five tabs: Chat, Council, Tasks, Agents, Settings.
- The app opens in its own window.
- Shorter docs: a one-page README and a one-page safety guide.
